Gentle Wake privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026.

Gentle Wake is developed by Adam Whiles. For support or privacy questions, contact adam@signalpeakmedia.com.

Information in the iPhone app

Gentle Wake does not collect personal information, listening history, advertising identifiers or analytics. It has no account system, advertising SDK, tracking SDK, microphone recording or developer-operated audio server. Sounds are generated on your device or played from audio files bundled with the app.

Your mixes, volume and settings stay on your device, subject to Apple's device backup and restore settings. Optional system backup alarms use Apple's AlarmKit with your permission; their identifier and scheduled time are stored locally. Microphone, contacts, location and health access are not required.

Purchases and the free trial

Apple handles the free trial activation, payment, purchase history and restoration through the App Store. Gentle Wake uses Apple's StoreKit to verify access on your device, including the original trial purchase date. The app does not send receipts, purchase identifiers or payment details to a developer server. Apple processes purchase and transaction information under its own privacy policy. Internet access is needed to activate, buy or restore access. Verified access and bundled audio can work offline.

Support and this website

If you email support, the information you choose to send, your email address and our replies are used to address your request. Do not send payment-card details or passwords. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve requests and meet legal obligations; contact us to request its deletion.

These public support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary connection information such as IP addresses and request logs under its privacy statement. The pages add no analytics, advertising, cookies, contact forms or third-party fonts. Website hosting practices are separate from the offline iPhone app.

Your choices

You can delete saved mixes in the app. Deleting the app removes app-local settings, subject to normal Apple backup behavior. Purchase history remains with Apple; reinstalling does not create a new three-day trial. Cancel any system backup alarm before removing the app. Apple may separately process diagnostics if you choose to share them in iOS settings. The developer does not add a crash-reporting SDK.

Changes

Material changes to the app's information practices will be reflected in this policy. The effective date above identifies this version.
