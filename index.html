Gentle Wake support

Sleep sounds, custom mixes and gentle wake schedules for iPhone.

Email adam@signalpeakmedia.com for help. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Gentle Wake version and what happened. Never send passwords or card information.

Start listening

Choose up to three sounds in Sound library, then use Sleep Setup to adjust levels and press Play. A selected sound can be tapped again to remove it. Save combinations in Saved mixes. The 0–100% master control covers the app's entire loudness range; your iPhone's media volume also affects loudness. Start low and use a comfortable level.

Set a wake schedule

Open Gentle wakeup, choose a duration or clock time and select Fade to silence or a wake sound. The fade finishes at the selected time. With a wake sound selected, the sleep audio fades down while the wake sound fades up. Choose how long it plays. Check the date and time shown for the next alarm.

Leave Gentle Wake running in the background; you may lock the screen. Do not force-close it. App/message alerts can mix with playback. Calls, Siri and other alarms may interrupt it; playback resumes when iOS permits. A disconnected audio route pauses playback so it does not unexpectedly play through the speaker.

Silent mode, Focus and backup alarms

Sleep and wake audio use your media output and volume. Test your setup before relying on it overnight. On iOS 26 or later, an optional system backup alarm can be armed with permission. It is a separate audible alert two minutes after the chosen wake time and can sound through Silent mode and Focus. It is off by default. The smooth wake transition itself requires the app's audio session to be running. Check your connected speakers/headphones, media volume and battery before bed.

Trial, purchase and restoration

The free trial lasts 72 hours from explicit activation. It never charges you automatically. A separate one-time purchase permanently unlocks the app; there is no subscription. The purchase screen displays your App Store's localized price.

After trial expiry, saved mixes remain available to browse. New sound sessions, previews and schedules require the unlock. A session already playing can continue for up to 12 hours after trial expiry, including its existing wake schedule. An already-armed system backup alarm remains separately managed and can be canceled.

Use Settings → Trial, unlock & restore → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used originally. Reinstalling does not restart the trial. Internet access is needed to activate, buy or restore. Bundled sounds and verified access work offline. If a purchase is pending approval, wait for Apple approval before trying again. For refunds, use Apple's refund service.
